# SkillSwap

**SkillSwap** is a collaborative mobile application that enables users to exchange skills without using money. Whether you're fluent in Spanish, great at graphic design, or know how to fix bikes, the app helps you connect with others looking for what you offer — and offering what you need. It's a peer-to-peer system that values learning and mutual support over financial transactions.

---

### The Problem

Many people have valuable skills but lack a convenient way to share and exchange them. Traditional paid services can be expensive or impersonal, and informal skill exchanges are difficult to organize.

---

### The Solution

**SkillSwap** transforms your knowledge into opportunity. By matching people based on complementary skills and facilitating the entire exchange process from discovery to completion, the app makes peer-to-peer learning accessible and sustainable.

---

### How It Works

1. Create your profile and list the skills you can offer, along with your proficiency level
2. Browse a personalized feed of requests from people seeking help in areas you know
3. Swipe right on requests you want to help with, or swipe left to skip
4. When a request owner accepts your offer, a chat opens so you can coordinate
5. Complete the exchange and rate each other to build your reputation

---

### Core Features

* **Skill-Based Matching Feed**
  A Tinder-style swipeable interface shows you requests that match your skill set. The recommendation engine learns from your interactions, prioritizing skills you frequently accept and filtering out those you tend to skip.

* **Real-Time Chat**
  Once both parties agree to an exchange, an in-app chat opens for coordination. Chats are organized into three tabs: Ongoing exchanges, Replies to your posts, and Pending requests you have made.

* **Smart Notifications**
  Push notifications keep you informed about new messages, incoming replies, and matching requests. The system is context-aware and suppresses notifications when you are already viewing a conversation.

* **Location Filtering**
  Set your preferred distance for in-person exchanges. The app uses your location to filter requests and show you opportunities nearby.

* **Rating System**
  After completing a skill exchange, both users rate each other. These ratings build your reputation over time and help the community identify reliable partners.

* **User Safety**
  Block users you do not want to interact with and report inappropriate posts. Heavily reported content is automatically filtered from the feed.

* **Media Support**
  Upload a profile picture and attach images to your skill requests to better communicate what you need or can offer.

---

### Technical Stack

The app follows MVVM architecture with the Repository pattern. It uses Firebase for authentication, database, cloud messaging, and storage. The UI is built entirely with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 design components.

---

### Getting Started

Sign in with Google or create an account with email and password. Add the skills you can offer by selecting from the available categories and setting your proficiency level. Then start browsing the feed or create your first request.
